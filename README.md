## About Dexter
*Dexter* follows Dexter Morgan, a blood-spatter analyst working with the Miami police. He maintains a carefully constructed routine that allows him to appear normal while hiding a separate life that no one around him knows about. The series focuses on how he manages his job, personal relationships, and the constant pressure of keeping his secret from people who investigate crimes for a living.

## The Appeal of Dexter
- Watching Dexter feels like walking on eggshells — you know what he's hiding, but no one around him does, and that unease follows you throughout.
- He spends every day surrounded by detectives who could expose him at any moment.

- There's something unsettling about watching him get close to people who genuinely care about him — knowing they're completely oblivious to the truth creates this uncomfortable sense of guilt.
- His bond with Debra is one of the show's strongest elements — they're incredibly close, and even after she learns the truth, she stands by him in her own way somehow.
- Watching him mimic normal behavior when he processes emotions differently makes every single conversation feel unsettling, like it could go wrong at any second.
